# Ababeel Fashions — Shopify theme

A mobile-first Shopify 2.0 theme shell for Ababeel Fashions, built around the existing Instagram-led brand and an India-wide checkout flow.

## Included

- Marketplace-inspired homepage with a utility bar, full-width search, category navigation, image-led category cards, store promises, a featured product grid, and a wedding-season banner
- Replaceable editorial starter images included for the initial preview; replace them with real Ababeel product photos before launch
- Footer-colour design system built around Ababeel's deep plum brand tone
- Product pages with image gallery, variant/size selection, add-to-cart, COD/delivery notes, and WhatsApp question CTA
- Collection grid with sorting and pagination
- Cart page with quantity controls, order note, checkout button, and WhatsApp support
- Search, page, and 404 templates
- Theme editor controls for logo, brand colors, social links, address, and WhatsApp number
- Responsive styling for mobile-first Instagram and Meta ad traffic
- Marketplace-style product discovery with collection filters, sorting, predictive search, quick add, wishlist saved in the browser, cart drawer, and collection list
- Brand story, Instagram showcase, email signup, size-guide template, structured business data, and accessible mobile navigation
- Starter editorial images included for the preview only; replace them with the real Ababeel catalogue and approved Instagram media before launch

## Upload to Shopify

1. In Shopify admin, open **Online Store → Themes**.
2. Choose **Add theme → Upload zip file**. Zip the contents of this `shopify-theme` directory so that `layout`, `sections`, `templates`, `snippets`, `assets`, `config`, and `locales` are at the top level of the zip.
3. Preview the uploaded theme before publishing.
4. In **Customize**, add the real Ababeel logo, hero/category photos, menu links, and a featured collection.
5. In **Products**, add products with clean titles, price, stock, size variants, fabric details, and 4–6 photos each.
6. In **Settings → Payments**, configure a valid India payment provider/UPI option and COD. Do not put gateway credentials into theme files.
7. In **Settings → Shipping and delivery**, configure India courier zones, rates, and COD rules.
8. Create and review the policy pages before launch: Shipping & delivery, Returns & exchanges, Privacy, Terms, and Contact.
9. Connect `ababeeltvm.com` only after the Shopify storefront and checkout are tested end-to-end.

## Product content checklist

For each product, prepare: product name, collection, price, compare-at price if any, sizes, measurements, fabric, lining, wash care, stock, delivery estimate, exchange eligibility, and front/back/detail photos. Use consistent filenames and include a short Malayalam phrase in the social caption if the product will also be promoted on Instagram.
